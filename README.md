# chocolate_sales_analysis
SQL & Excel analysis of chocolate sales data, exploring revenue, pricing, and demand across markets. The project identifies key drivers of performance, compares market behavior, and provides insights on pricing strategies and product performance.

Objectives

Identify top-performing markets and products;
Analyze price vs demand relationship;
Compare market behavior;
Evaluate overall product performance;

🛠 Tools Used
SQL (data cleaning & analysis)
Excel (visualization & dashboard)

📊 Key Insights

Lower prices generally drive higher order volume;
USA shows high pricing but lower demand;
Canada has strong demand but underpriced products;
New Zealand is the weakest market due to low demand;
No strong seasonality — demand remains stable;
